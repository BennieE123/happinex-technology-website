Excel for Data Analysis

Turn Raw Data Into Meaningful Insights

Learn how to use Microsoft Excel to clean, organise, analyse and visualise data for better decision-making. Develop practical skills through hands-on exercises and real-world data analysis activities.

About This Course

The Excel for Data Analysis course at Happinex Academy is designed to equip learners with practical skills for working with data using Microsoft Excel.

Rather than focusing only on basic spreadsheet operations, this course explores how Excel can be used to clean, organise, transform, analyse and present data to support informed business decisions.

What You Will Learn

Data Cleaning and Preparation

Identify and correct errors, duplicates, missing values and inconsistencies in datasets.

Excel Functions and Formulas

Apply essential and advanced functions to perform calculations and analyse data.

Data Analysis with PivotTables

Summarise, group and explore large datasets to uncover useful information.

Data Visualisation

Create meaningful charts and visual representations of data.

Interactive Dashboards

Build dashboards that communicate key performance indicators and business insights.

Business Insights

Interpret analytical results and communicate findings to support decision-making.

Who Should Enrol?

This course is suitable for individuals who want to develop practical data analysis skills using Microsoft Excel.

  • Beginners interested in starting a career in data analysis.
  • Students and graduates seeking practical digital skills.
  • Professionals who work with spreadsheets and business data.
  • Business owners who want to make better use of their data.
  • Anyone interested in improving their analytical skills.

Learn Through Practical Experience

At Happinex Academy, learning goes beyond theory. You will work with practical datasets, complete guided exercises and apply Excel techniques to solve realistic data analysis problems.

By the end of the course, you will have practical experience preparing datasets, analysing information and presenting findings through meaningful reports and dashboards.

What You Will Be Able to Do

  • Clean and prepare datasets for analysis.
  • Use Excel formulas and functions to solve analytical problems.
  • Summarise and interpret data using PivotTables.
  • Create charts and interactive dashboards.
  • Identify patterns and trends in business data.
  • Communicate findings to support informed decisions.
