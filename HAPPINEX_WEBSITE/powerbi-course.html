Power BI for Business Intelligence and Visualization

Transform Data Into Interactive Business Insights

Learn how to connect, transform, model and analyse data using Microsoft Power BI. Develop the skills to create interactive reports and dashboards that help organisations make informed decisions.

About This Course

The Power BI for Business Intelligence and Visualization course at Happinex Academy is designed to equip learners with practical skills for transforming raw data into meaningful business intelligence.

Learners will explore how to import data from different sources, clean and transform datasets, create data relationships, perform calculations using DAX and build interactive dashboards that communicate valuable insights.

What You Will Learn

Introduction to Power BI

Understand Power BI Desktop, its interface and its role in business intelligence.

Data Import and Transformation

Connect to data sources and clean, transform and prepare data using Power Query.

Data Modelling

Create relationships between tables and organise data for effective analysis.

DAX Calculations

Use Data Analysis Expressions (DAX) to create calculated columns and measures.

Data Visualisation

Choose appropriate charts and visual elements to communicate analytical findings.

Interactive Dashboards

Build reports with slicers, filters, KPI cards and interactive visualisations.

Who Should Enrol?

This course is suitable for individuals who want to develop practical business intelligence and data visualisation skills.

  • Aspiring data analysts and business intelligence professionals.
  • Excel users who want to advance into Power BI.
  • Business owners and managers interested in data-driven decisions.
  • Students and graduates seeking practical analytical skills.
  • Professionals who prepare reports and business dashboards.

Learn Through Practical Experience

At Happinex Academy, you will work with practical datasets and guided exercises to understand how Power BI is applied in real analytical situations.

You will practise importing and transforming data, developing data models, writing DAX measures and building interactive reports that communicate business performance and insights.

Your learning experience will focus on applying Power BI techniques to answer business questions and support informed decision-making.

What You Will Be Able to Do

  • Connect Power BI to different data sources.
  • Clean and transform datasets using Power Query.
  • Build data models and establish relationships.
  • Create calculations and measures using DAX.
  • Design interactive reports and dashboards.
  • Present business insights through meaningful visualisations.
